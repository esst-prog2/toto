Club-identity groups and all 394 round members are traced in metadata but are **not** counted as individually corroborated fixtures.

`VERIFIED` means an independently authored narrative or official record directly inspected supports date, home club, away club and score. It does not mean every later appeal was searched or legal finality established. `PARTIAL` means at least one such fact is supported, or reliable evidence contradicts a source fact. `UNRESOLVED` includes index-only corroboration, unavailable documents, and database agreement with unestablished upstream independence. Unresolved is not synonymous with wrong.

A round is `DOCUMENTED` when a directly inspected independent/official document explains a relevant event or reprogramming; that does not certify all its results or prove a scheduled game was actually completed. `PARTIAL` includes indexed explanations and cases with only some outliers documented. All 31 partial contexts remain open in that sense; zero contexts without a research lead does **not** mean zero remaining scheduling uncertainties.

## Reproducible selection and evidence accounting

Before online research, freeze one ordinary nonflagged record per represented season. In older seasons alternate home-win/draw strata by `(start_year-1990)%2`; all older numeric away wins carry the known label flag. In modern seasons rotate home/draw/away by `(start_year-2014)%3`. Within each stratum select the minimum SHA-256 of UTF-8 `ligue1-phase5-v1:` plus the immutable audit ID. No substitution is made when evidence is hard to find. Additional away-label examples are the first chronological PSG away victory in each of the 1990s, 2000s and 2010s within the older primary window, chosen from source values before lookup.

The private ledger records full source path, file SHA-256, physical source line, raw-line SHA-256, source-bound audit ID and original date/teams/score. These audit IDs are not future standardized match IDs. Literal fixture-specific renderings of evidence team names are not global normalization rules. Supplemental major-case CSV bindings are reparsed and verified against the immutable original. Every round preserves its exact Phase 4 member set and every member's source binding.

The offline checker rejects changed bindings, duplicate IDs/JSON keys, dropped samples or rounds, undocumented upgrades of indexed/shared evidence, blanket round certification, and attempted Phase 5 date/label/supplement implementation. It checks bookkeeping and claim strength, not whether an online report was correct. Human evidence judgements cannot be reproduced merely by rerunning Python; the ledger freezes exactly what was inspected and its limits.

## Systematic auxiliary result-label defect

Phase 4's unchanged full-CSV diagnosis is 5,489 contradictions; 1,895 fall in the older primary window. Every contradiction has numeric away-win goals but auxiliary `D`, before 2015/16. Home wins and true draws do not show this pattern; later away wins use `A`. Numeric full-time goals, total goals and goal differences agree internally throughout the CSV, but internal agreement cannot establish historical truth.

The independent ordinary sample supports home wins and draws in the 1990s, 2000s and later decades. The extra 2000-10-21 Toulouse–PSG 2–3 example is supported by a directly inspected signed narrative describing the goal sequence, while the source labels it `D`. The 1991-01-20 Auxerre–PSG 0–1 compiler and 2010-09-26 Lens–PSG 0–2 contemporary index remain unresolved under the stricter independence/access rule. Evidence therefore supports a defective auxiliary encoder hypothesis, without proving its implementation cause or certifying all numeric away scores.

Propose for Phase 6: derive H/D/A from accepted numeric goals **only after** separate administrative-result and eligibility review. In particular, a tied numeric score can accompany an awarded winner. No source label or result was changed here.

### Missing Nantes–Toulouse 2006/07

Directly inspected [Conseil d’État decision 308561, 4 April 2008](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/ceta/id/CETATEXT000019427811) establishes the 87th-minute permanent interruption, penalty defeat and three Toulouse points on 24 May 2007, registration on 25 May, FFF appeal confirmation on 12 June, and season homologation on 14 June. The judicial challenge was rejected. A directly inspected [contemporary report](https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/Actualites/le-conseil-d-etat-valide-nantes-tfc/800000000000000000000000025572) identifies the event as 19 May 2007 at Beaujoire, stopped at 0–0. Its publication is 14 April 2008 but its relative weekday for the court ruling is inconsistent with the court record; use the latter for the judicial date.

These establish an actual interrupted fixture and awarded Toulouse victory, **not** an official 0–3 numerical score. Three awarded points are not three awarded goals. The club retrospective agrees on on-field 0–0 and Toulouse's points. A future supplemental audit record is evidence-supported in principle, but a screened ordinary draw or invented numeric award is not justified. Seek the original registration/appeal text and approve supplementation separately; no record was added.

## Club identities and source boundary

[BnF authority FRBNF12335812](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb123358120) identifies Stade brestois / FC Brest Armorique names, relegation after 1990/91, insolvency in October 1991 and liquidation in 1992, followed by Stade brestois 29. It supports a **season-bounded sporting-entrant** reconciliation of the two 1990/91 labels. It does not justify a global legal-entity merger across dissolution and succession. All 38 target source fixtures stay as supplied.

The [FFF Gazélec history](https://www.fff.fr/216-parcours-national-ajaccio-gfc.html) explicitly places GFC Ajaccio in Ligue 1 in 2015/16. The primary modern source uses Gazélec; the overlapping CSV uses AC Ajaccio. Those are distinct clubs, not interchangeable abbreviations. Keep all 38 unmatched pairings on each side explicit; propose preferring the modern source's season entrant after approval, never an AC Ajaccio→Gazélec global mapping.

The [current Toulouse club history](https://www.toulousefc.com/region/histoire) presents 1937 heritage alongside the modern era; indexed historical narrative distinguishes the old club absorbed in 1967 and the 1970 restart. Legal affiliation continuity remains insufficiently established for a global multi-decade mapping. All target-window Toulouse records belong to the modern era, but that does not resolve older identical labels. Other reconstituted clubs and Paris entities retain the Phase 4 continuity warning; spelling similarity alone is insufficient evidence.

The 2014/15 source-boundary overlap and eleven scored overlap windows are preserved unchanged. Agreement is diagnostic consistency, not independent historical verification. The 2015/16 Ajaccio mismatch and Bastia numeric conflict remain explicit.
